What is Total Profit?

Total Profit is the amount earned after subtracting your original investment from the current value of your portfolio.

Measure Your Investment Success

Total Profit helps investors understand how much wealth has been created through appreciation,dividends,interest,and capital gains.

Tracking profit regularly allows you to compare investments and make informed financial decisions for future growth.

Quick Formula

Total Profit=Current Portfolio Value−Total Investment Amount
