What Are Mutual Funds?

Mutual funds pool money from multiple investors and invest in diversified assets managed by professional fund managers.

Professionally Managed Investments

Mutual funds offer diversification,professional management and easy access to equity,debt,hybrid,index and sector-specific investments.

They are ideal for investors seeking long-term wealth creation through disciplined investing,especially via SIPs.

Investment Tip

Continue monthly SIPs and stay invested to benefit from rupee cost averaging and compounding.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is NAV?

NAV(Net Asset Value)represents the per-unit value of a mutual fund.

Is SIP better than lump sum?

SIP helps average purchase costs and reduces the impact of market volatility.

When should I redeem my funds?

Redeem when you achieve your financial goals or your asset allocation needs rebalancing.
