What is an OTP Scam?

An OTP scam occurs when a fraudster tricks a person into revealing the One-Time Password sent by the bank or payment application.Once shared,the scammer can complete unauthorized transactions.

OTP Protects Your Money

Banks send OTPs only to verify that you are authorizing a transaction.

Anyone asking for your OTP is attempting to gain access to your account.

Remember

Banks,RBI,NPCI,police or customer care never ask for your OTP.

Real Scam Examples

These are common OTP scams reported across India.

Fake Bank Call

Fraudsters pretend to be bank officials and ask for Your OTP to "verify your account."

KYC Update Scam

Victims receive calls claiming their KYC has expired and are asked to share an OTP.

Lottery Prize Scam

Scammers promise cash prizes and request an OTP to "claim" the reward.

Courier Delivery Scam

Fake delivery agents ask for an OTP saying it is required to deliver your parcel.

How OTP Scams Work

Fraudsters use fear,urgency and trust to convince victims to reveal their OTPs.

Fake Calls

Scammers pretend to be bank officials or customer support executives.

Fake SMS

Fraud messages contain fake links asking you to verify your account.

Identity Theft

Criminals impersonate trusted organizations to gain your confidence.

Urgency

Victims are told to act immediately or their account will be blocked.

Warning Signs

Stop immediately if you notice any of these red flags.

Someone Asks for OTP

Banks never ask customers to share OTPs over phone,SMS or email.

Creates Panic

Messages saying your account will be blocked immediately are usually fraudulent.

Unknown Links

Avoid clicking suspicious banking links received through SMS or WhatsApp.

How to Stay Safe from OTP Scams

Follow these simple practices to keep your bank account,digital wallet and personal information secure.

Never share your OTP with anyone,even if they claim to be from your bank or a government organization.

Read SMS messages carefully.Banks clearly mention that OTPs should never be shared.

Disconnect suspicious calls immediately and contact your bank using the official customer care number.

Enable SMS and email alerts for all banking transactions to detect unauthorized activity quickly.

Common Mistakes People Make

Avoid these mistakes to reduce the chances of becoming an OTP scam victim.

Sharing OTP Over Phone

Believing fake callers and revealing the OTP received on your mobile.

Clicking Fake Links

Opening unknown banking links received through SMS,email or WhatsApp.

Trusting Unknown People

Assuming every caller is a genuine bank employee without verification.

Ignoring Warning Messages

Banks always mention "Do not share your OTP" in the SMS,but many users ignore it.

What To Do If You Shared Your OTP

Act immediately to minimize financial loss.

Call Your Bank

Request immediate blocking of your account,card or online banking services.

Change Passwords

Reset internet banking,UPI and mobile banking passwords immediately.

Report Cyber Crime

File a complaint through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal as soon as possible.

Save Evidence

Keep SMS messages,call recordings,transaction details and screenshots safely.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a bank employee ask for my OTP?

No.Genuine bank employees never ask customers to reveal OTPs.

Is it safe to share OTP with customer care?

No.OTP is strictly confidential and should never be shared with anyone.

What should I do if I accidentally shared my OTP?

Contact your bank immediately,block your account if required and report the incident to cybercrime authorities.
