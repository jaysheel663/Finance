Daily Security Checklist

Follow these simple steps every day.

Keep your Debit/Credit Card with you.

Never share your PIN, CVV or OTP.

Enable SMS & Email transaction alerts.

Review account statements regularly.

Use trusted ATMs and secure websites.

Lock your card immediately if lost.

Use strong passwords for banking apps.

Report suspicious activity immediately.

Emergency Actions

Block Card

Disable your card immediately after loss.

Call Bank

Contact customer support 24×7.

Report Fraud

Raise an official fraud complaint.
