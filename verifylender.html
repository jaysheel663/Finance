Verifying lenders protects you from fake loan providers,identity theft,illegal recovery practices,and advance payment scams.

Verification Checklist

Always complete these checks before applying for an online loan.

Check RBI Registration

Confirm whether the lender is an RBI-approved bank or NBFC.

Visit Official Website

Verify company details using the official website only.

Read Customer Reviews

Check trusted reviews and ratings before applying.

Review Loan Terms

Read interest rates, fees, repayment schedule, and policies.

Warning Signs of Fake Lenders

Avoid loan providers displaying any of these warning signs.

Advance Fee Request

Demands payment before loan approval.

Requests Sensitive Data

Asks for OTPs, PINs, or banking passwords.

No Customer Support

No verified contact information or office address.

Untrusted App

App is unavailable on official app stores.

Follow these best practices to ensure secure borrowing.

Protect Personal Information

Install Trusted Apps

Download loan apps only from verified app stores.

Know Your Rights
