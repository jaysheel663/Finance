Real Card Fraud Cases

Learn from actual fraud incidents,understand how scammers fooled victims,identify warning signs,and discover how these attacks could have been prevented.

Popular Fraud Cases

Real incidents that happened to banking customers.

ATM Skimming

Fraudsters installed hidden devices to steal card details and PINs from ATM users.

Fake Banking App

Customers downloaded fake banking apps that captured login credentials.

Phishing Emails

Victims clicked fake bank emails and unknowingly revealed card information.

Fake Customer Care

Scammers posed as bank officials and convinced customers to share OTPs.

Warning Signs

Recognize suspicious behaviour before becoming a victim.

Urgent Calls

Fraudsters create panic and ask for immediate action.

OTP Requests

No genuine bank employee asks for your OTP or PIN.

Unknown Links

Avoid clicking links received through SMS or emails.

Fake Websites

Always verify website URLs before entering card details.
