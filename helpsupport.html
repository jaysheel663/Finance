Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions.

How do I reset my password?

Navigate to Security Settings and select "Change Password".Follow the instructions to update your account password securely.

How can I update my profile information?

Go to your Profile page and click "Edit Profile" to update personal and financial details.

How do I track my expenses?

Use the Budget Tracker page to record income and expenses and monitor spending patterns.

How can I enable Two-Factor Authentication?

Open Security Settings and enable Two-Factor Authentication for additional account protection.

Is my financial information secure?

Yes.Your information is protected using secure authentication methods and privacy controls.

How do I create a savings goal?

Visit the Budget Tracker or Goal section and create a savings target with a timeline.

Can I export my financial reports?

Yes.Reports can be exported for personal analysis and record keeping.

How do I report suspicious activity?

Immediately contact support through the Security Support section and change your password if necessary.

How does the Financial Calculator work?

Enter financial values such as investment amount,interest rate or tenure and the calculator will generate results instantly.

Can I access FinanceHub on mobile devices?

Yes.The platform is fully responsive and optimized for mobile,tablet and desktop use.
