What is Investment Return?

Investment Return is the percentage gain or loss earned on your invested money over a specific period.

Measure Portfolio Performance

Returns indicate how efficiently your investments have generated wealth over time.

Investors use return percentage to compare different investment options and evaluate financial performance.

Return Formula

Return(%)=(Profit ÷ Investment)×100

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good investment return?

It depends on the asset type,but a long-term return of 10–15% is generally considered very good for equity-based investments.

Why do returns fluctuate?

Market movements,economic conditions and asset prices change daily,affecting investment returns.

How can I earn higher returns?

Invest regularly,diversify your portfolio and stay invested for the long term.
