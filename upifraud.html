What is UPI Fraud?

UPI fraud happens when scammers trick users into sending money or revealing confidential banking information through fake payment requests,QR codes,links or phone calls.

Think Before You Click

Fraudsters pretend to be bank officials,customer support executives,buyers,sellers or government representatives to gain your trust.

Remember that entering your UPI PIN always means you are authorizing a payment from your account.

Golden Rule

Receiving money never requires entering your UPI PIN.

Real Fraud Examples

These are some of the most commonly reported UPI fraud methods in India.

Fake QR Code Scam

A scammer sends a QR code claiming you'll receive money.After scanning and entering your PIN,money is deducted instead.

Fake Customer Care Call

Fraudsters pretend to be customer support and ask you to install screen sharing apps or reveal banking details.

Online Marketplace Scam

A fake buyer says they have sent payment and requests you to approve the transaction.

Fake Payment Link

Clicking unknown payment links may redirect you to fake websites designed to steal banking information.

How UPI Fraud Happens

Most UPI frauds succeed because scammers manipulate people's trust rather than hacking bank systems.

Social Engineering

Fraudsters emotionally convince victims to reveal confidential information.

Fake Payment Requests

Victims unknowingly approve payment requests believing they are receiving money.

Remote Access Apps

Apps like screen-sharing software are misused to control victims' phones.

Fake Websites

Fraudsters create fake banking pages to steal login credentials.

Warning Signs

Stop immediately if you notice any of these suspicious activities.

Asking for UPI PIN

No bank or UPI company will ever ask for your UPI PIN.

Scan to Receive Money

Receiving money never requires scanning a QR code.

Urgent Pressure

Scammers often create panic by saying your account will be blocked immediately.

How to Stay Safe from UPI Fraud

Following a few simple safety practices can protect your money and personal information from fraudsters.

Never share your UPI PIN,OTP,debit card details or CVV with anyone.

Carefully read every UPI notification before tapping "Pay" or entering your UPI PIN.

Download banking and UPI applications only from Google Play Store or Apple App Store.

Immediately report any suspicious transaction to your bank and cybercrime authorities.

Common Mistakes People Make

Most UPI frauds happen because of these avoidable mistakes.

Scanning Unknown QR Codes

Never scan a QR code sent by strangers claiming you will receive money.

Sharing UPI PIN

Your UPI PIN should remain secret,just like your ATM PIN.

Installing Unknown Apps

Avoid installing APK files or remote access apps sent through WhatsApp or SMS.

Trusting Fake Calls

Never believe callers who claim to be from your bank without verification.

What To Do Immediately After a Fraud

Acting quickly increases the chances of recovering your money.

Call Your Bank

Inform your bank and request them to block further transactions.

Block UPI Services

Temporarily disable UPI transactions through your banking app.

Report Cyber Crime

File a complaint through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal or helpline immediately.

Keep Evidence

Save screenshots,transaction IDs,messages and call details for investigation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can someone steal money just by knowing my UPI ID?

No.Your UPI ID alone is not enough.Transactions require your approval and UPI PIN.

Is it safe to share my QR code?

Yes.Sharing your personal QR code to receive money is generally safe,but never scan unknown QR codes.

Can banks ask for my UPI PIN or OTP?

Never.Banks,NPCI and UPI apps will never ask for your PIN or OTP.
