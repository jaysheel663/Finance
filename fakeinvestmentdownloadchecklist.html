Report Investment Fraud

If you have encountered a fake investment scheme or suspect fraudulent financial activity,report it immediately to help protect yourself and others from financial scams.

Why Report Fraud?

Reporting fraudulent investment schemes helps authorities investigate scammers and prevents others from becoming victims.

Protect Yourself

Reporting quickly may reduce further financial losses.

Protect Others

Your report can prevent others from investing in fake schemes.

Support Investigation

Authorities can investigate and take legal action against fraudsters.

Steps to Report Fraud

Follow these simple steps to submit your complaint.

Collect Evidence

Save payment receipts,emails,messages,screenshots and transaction details.

Document Details

Record the company name,website,contact numbers and investment offers.

Submit Complaint

Report the fraud through official financial regulators or cybercrime portals.

Track Status

Keep your complaint reference number for future follow-up.

Information Required

Prepare the following details before submitting your report.

Company Details

Company name,website,registration number and office address.

Transaction Details

Investment amount,payment method and transaction reference numbers.

Supporting Evidence

Upload screenshots,emails,SMS messages and payment receipts.

Your Information

Provide accurate contact details for investigation updates.

Common Scam Indicators

Recognize these warning signs before investing.

Guaranteed Returns

Promises of huge profits with zero investment risk.

Pressure to Invest

Urgent deadlines encouraging immediate payment.

Hidden Identity

No genuine company registration or verified contact details.

Emergency Assistance

Contact the appropriate authorities immediately if you suspect fraud.

Financial Regulator

Report suspicious investment companies to financial regulatory authorities.

Cyber Crime Portal

File online complaints for digital investment fraud and cyber scams.

Customer Support

Contact your bank immediately if unauthorized transactions occurred.
