What is ATM/Debit Card Fraud?

ATM or debit card fraud occurs when criminals steal your card information or PIN and use it to withdraw cash or make unauthorized purchases without your permission.

Protect Your Card Information

Criminals use skimming devices,hidden cameras and fake payment machines to copy card details.

They later clone the card or use stolen information for online transactions.

Remember

Cover the keypad while entering your PIN and inspect the ATM before using it.

Real ATM/Debit Card Fraud Examples

These are some of the most common debit card frauds reported across India.

ATM Skimming

Hidden skimming devices copy card information while customers use the ATM.

Hidden Camera Scam

Tiny cameras secretly record customers entering their ATM PIN.

Card Cloning

Stolen card details are copied onto duplicate cards for unauthorized cash withdrawals.

Fake Bank Calls

Fraudsters pretend to be bank officials and ask for card numbers,CVV and PIN.

How ATM/Debit Card Fraud Works

Fraudsters use different techniques to steal your card details and PIN before making unauthorized transactions.

Card Skimming

A fake card reader is placed over the ATM card slot to secretly copy card information.

PIN Recording

Hidden cameras or fake keypads record the PIN entered by customers.

Card Cloning

Stolen card information is copied onto another card and used for ATM withdrawals or purchases.

Unauthorized Transactions

Fraudsters withdraw cash or shop online before the victim notices the fraud.

Warning Signs

Watch for these signs before using an ATM or sharing card information.

Loose Card Slot

If the card reader looks unusual or loose,avoid using that ATM.

Hidden Cameras

Check around the keypad for tiny cameras or suspicious objects.

Calls Asking Card Details

Banks never ask for your PIN,CVV or OTP over phone calls.

How to Stay Safe from ATM/Debit Card Fraud

Following these simple safety practices can help protect your debit card,PIN and bank account from fraudsters.

Always cover the keypad while entering your ATM PIN to prevent hidden cameras from recording it.

Inspect the ATM card slot and keypad for unusual attachments before using the machine.

Enable SMS and mobile banking alerts to receive instant notifications for every card transaction.

Keep your debit card,PIN,CVV and OTP completely confidential at all times.

Common Mistakes People Make

Many debit card frauds happen because users unknowingly make these mistakes.

Exposing Your PIN

Entering the PIN without covering the keypad allows others to observe it.

Ignoring ATM Changes

Using ATMs with damaged or unusual-looking card slots increases fraud risk.

Sharing Card Details

Giving card number,CVV,OTP or PIN to unknown callers pretending to be bank officials.

Ignoring Alerts

Not checking SMS or mobile banking notifications after every transaction.

What To Do If Your Card Is Misused

Act immediately to reduce financial losses and secure your account.

Block Your Card

Immediately block your debit card using mobile banking,internet banking or customer care.

Inform Your Bank

Report unauthorized transactions and request an investigation.

Report Cyber Crime

Lodge a complaint with the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal and local police.

Save Transaction Details

Preserve SMS alerts,ATM receipts,bank statements and screenshots for future investigation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can banks ask for my ATM PIN or CVV?

No.Banks never ask for your ATM PIN,CVV or OTP through calls,SMS or emails.

What should I do if my ATM card is lost?

Immediately block your debit card and inform your bank to prevent unauthorized use.

Is it safe to save my card details on shopping websites?

Save card details only on trusted platforms that provide strong security and two-factor authentication.
