UPI Fraud Reporting

Report UPI Fraud

If you notice an unauthorized UPI transaction or suspect a payment scam,record the details and report the incident promptly.

Keep These Details Ready

Useful information to provide when reporting suspicious activity.

Transaction Amount

Record the amount involved in the suspicious transaction.

Date & Time

Note when the transaction or suspicious contact occurred.

Transaction Reference

Keep the transaction or payment reference number available.

Scammer Details

Save relevant phone numbers, messages, profiles, or payment IDs.

Immediate Actions

Take appropriate action as soon as you notice suspected UPI fraud.

Contact Your Bank

Inform your bank about the suspicious transaction and follow its instructions for securing your account.

Report Through the Payment App

Use the official complaint or transaction-support option available in your payment application.

Preserve Evidence

Keep screenshots,transaction details,messages,and other relevant information.

Use Official Fraud Channels

Report suspected fraud through the appropriate official financial or cybercrime reporting channel.

Act Quickly

If money has been transferred without your authorization,contact your bank and the relevant official reporting channels as soon as possible.
