Loan App Fraud Protection

Learn how fake loan applications operate,identify warning signs,protect your personal information,and safely apply for loans using trusted financial institutions.

What is Loan App Fraud?

Fraudulent loan applications promise instant loans with little documentation but steal personal information,demand advance payments,or misuse your banking details.

Fake Loan Apps

Fraudsters create fake apps that imitate trusted lenders.

Identity Theft

Personal information may be stolen and misused.

Advance Fee Scam

Victims are asked to pay processing fees before receiving loans.

Common Warning Signs

Watch for these indicators before installing or using any loan application.

Advance Payment

Legitimate lenders never demand loan approval fees upfront.

Excessive Permissions

Apps requesting contacts, gallery, SMS, or microphone unnecessarily.

Poor Reviews

Negative ratings and fraud complaints from users.

No Official Website

Fake lenders often lack verified company information.

How to Stay Safe

Follow these best practices before applying for online loans.

Use RBI Registered Lenders

Apply only through regulated financial institutions.

Verify the Company

Check official websites and genuine customer reviews.

Protect Personal Data

Never share OTPs, passwords or banking PINs.
