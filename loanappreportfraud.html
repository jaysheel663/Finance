Report Loan App Fraud

If you have been targeted by a fake loan application,report the incident immediately.Quick reporting helps protect your finances,supports investigations,and prevents scammers from targeting other people.

Why Report Loan App Fraud?

Reporting suspicious loan applications allows authorities and financial institutions to investigate fraudulent activities and protect future victims.

Protect Yourself

Reduce additional financial loss by reporting immediately.

Protect Others

Help prevent scammers from targeting more people.

Support Investigation

Authorities can take faster action against fraudulent lenders.

Common Loan App Fraud

Select the fraud category that best matches your situation.

Advance Fee Scam

Asked to pay processing charges before loan approval.

Identity Theft

Misuse of Aadhaar, PAN, or banking information.

Contact Harassment

Loan apps threatening family or friends using contact lists.

Fake Loan App

Fraudulent mobile application pretending to be a lender.

How to Report

Follow these steps while submitting your complaint.

Collect Evidence

Save screenshots, chats, receipts, and transaction details.

Fill Complaint

Provide complete and accurate information.

Submit Report

Send the complaint through official reporting channels.

Track Status

Monitor complaint updates using your reference ID.

Information Required

Prepare these details before reporting the fraud.

Your Information

Name, mobile number and email address.

Loan App Details

App name, website, company name or mobile number.

Transaction Details

Amount paid, payment mode and transaction reference.

Evidence

Screenshots, payment receipts and conversations.

Immediate Safety Measures

Take these actions immediately after discovering the fraud.

Block Bank Account

Contact your bank and secure your accounts.

Change Passwords

Update banking passwords and enable 2FA.

Contact Cybercrime

Report the incident to the official cybercrime portal.
