What is a Fixed Deposit?

A Fixed Deposit(FD) is a secure investment offered by banks and financial institutions where you deposit money for a fixed period and earn a guaranteed rate of interest.

Safe and Stable Investment

Fixed Deposits provide guaranteed returns and are ideal for conservative investors who prefer stability over market-linked investments.

The interest rate remains fixed throughout the tenure,making returns predictable.

Did You Know?

Fixed Deposits are one of the safest investment options because your returns are not affected by stock market fluctuations.

Types of Fixed Deposits

Banks offer different types of Fixed Deposits to suit various financial needs.

Regular FD

Standard Fixed Deposit for all investors.

Senior Citizen FD

Higher interest rates for senior citizens.

Tax Saver FD

Eligible for tax benefits with a five-year lock-in.

Cumulative FD

Interest is reinvested and paid at maturity.

Advantages of Fixed Deposits

Fixed Deposits provide safety,guaranteed returns and financial stability,making them suitable for conservative investors.

Capital Protection

Your invested amount remains safe throughout the investment period.

Guaranteed Returns

Interest rates remain fixed for the entire deposit tenure.

Flexible Tenure

Choose investment periods from a few days up to ten years.

Stable Income

Monthly,quarterly or maturity payouts are available.

Risk Analysis

Fixed Deposits are among the safest investment options,but they still have a few limitations.

Very Low Risk

Returns are fixed and unaffected by stock market fluctuations.

Inflation Risk

Inflation may reduce the real purchasing power of your returns.

Liquidity Risk

Premature withdrawal may result in penalty charges.

How to Open a Fixed Deposit?

Opening a Fixed Deposit is simple and can be completed online or by visiting your preferred bank branch.

Choose a bank or financial institution offering attractive FD interest rates.

Decide the investment amount and select the preferred tenure.

Select the payout option such as monthly,quarterly or maturity payment.

Complete the deposit and safely keep the FD receipt until maturity.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Avoid these common mistakes while investing in Fixed Deposits.

Ignoring Interest Rates

Compare interest rates before choosing a bank or institution.

Breaking FD Early

Premature withdrawal may reduce your earned interest.

Ignoring Inflation

Fixed returns may not always beat inflation over long periods.

Ignoring Tax Rules

Interest earned from Fixed Deposits is usually taxable.
