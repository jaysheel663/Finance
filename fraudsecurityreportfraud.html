Why Report Fraud?

Reporting fraud immediately helps reduce financial losses,prevents future attacks,and supports law enforcement investigations.

Protect Your Money

Quick reporting helps reduce additional financial damage.

Protect Others

Your report helps stop fraudsters from targeting more victims.

Support Investigation

Authorities can investigate fraudulent activities more efficiently.

Identity Theft

Reporting Process

Complete the following steps when submitting a fraud report.

Collect Evidence

Save screenshots, emails, receipts and transaction details.

Complete Report

Provide accurate information regarding the incident.

Submit Report

Send your report securely to the appropriate authority.

Track Progress

Monitor the complaint status using your reference number.

Information Required

Prepare the following details before submitting your report.

Personal Details

Name, contact number and email address.

Fraud Source

Company, website, mobile number or social media account.

Transaction Details

Amount, payment method and transaction reference.

Supporting Evidence

Upload screenshots, emails and payment receipts.

Immediate Actions

Take these steps immediately after detecting fraud.

Block Cards

Temporarily block affected cards or accounts.

Change Passwords

Update passwords and enable two-factor authentication.

Contact Bank

Inform your financial institution immediately.
