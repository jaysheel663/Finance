What is Total Investment?

Total Investment represents the combined amount of money you have invested across all financial assets.

Your Financial Foundation

Total investment is one of the most important financial indicators.It represents every rupee invested into different financial instruments for wealth creation and long-term growth.

Instead of looking at individual investments separately,total investment provides a complete view of your financial commitment.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Total Investment?

It is the total amount of money invested across all your financial assets such as stocks,mutual funds,gold and fixed deposits.

How often should I review my investments?

Reviewing your portfolio every three to six months is generally sufficient for long-term investors.

Why should investments be diversified?

Diversification helps reduce risk because poor performance in one asset can be balanced by others.
