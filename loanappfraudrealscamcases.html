Case Studies

Common loan application frauds reported by victims.

Advance Processing Fee Scam

Victims were promised instant loans after paying a processing fee,but no loan was ever disbursed.

Contact List Blackmail

Fraudulent loan apps accessed users' contacts and threatened to send fake repayment messages unless money was paid.

Identity Theft

Scammers collected Aadhaar,PAN,and bank details to commit financial fraud using victims' identities.

Fake Loan Approval

Users received fake approval notifications and were asked to pay insurance or GST charges before receiving funds.

Account Takeover

Victims unknowingly shared OTPs,allowing scammers to access their banking accounts.

Privacy Abuse

Loan apps misused camera,gallery,and microphone permissions to intimidate borrowers.

Lessons Learned

Follow these important lessons from real scam victims.

Never Pay Before Approval

Legitimate lenders never ask for advance payments.

Install Only Trusted Apps

Download loan apps only from verified companies.

Protect Personal Information

Never share OTPs or sensitive banking information.

Need Help?

If you experience a similar incident,report it immediately to your bank and cybercrime authorities.

Contact Your Bank

Inform your bank immediately if you suspect fraud.

Report to Cybercrime

File a complaint through official cybercrime portals.

Keep Evidence

Save screenshots, payment proofs, and chat history.
