UPI Safety

UPI Safety Guide

Follow these simple practices to make your UPI payments safer and reduce the risk of fraud.

Essential UPI Safety Rules

Check these rules before approving a UPI transaction.

01

Protect Your UPI PIN

Never share your UPI PIN with anyone,including customer-support representatives or callers.

02

Verify the Recipient

Confirm the recipient's name and payment details before authorizing a transaction.

03

Use QR Codes Carefully

Don't scan unknown QR codes when someone claims they will send money to you.

04

Ignore Fake Support

Never follow payment instructions from an unknown caller claiming to be support staff.

05

Check Payment Requests

Read the request carefully before approving it.A request may ask you to send money rather than receive it.

06

Keep Your App Secure

Use official payment applications and keep your phone,operating system,and applications updated.

07

Don't Trust Screenshots

Confirm money has actually reached your account instead of relying on screenshots or messages from another person.

08

Stop Under Pressure

If someone pressures you to make a quick payment,stop and independently verify the request.

Before Entering Your UPI PIN

Confirm the recipient,amount,and purpose of the transaction.If anything looks unfamiliar,cancel and verify first.

Think You Have Been Scammed?

Record the transaction details and report the suspicious activity.
