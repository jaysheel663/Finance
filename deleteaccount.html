Permanent Account Deletion

Deleting your FinanceHub account will permanently erase your financial information,investment records,budgeting history,reports and personalized settings.This actioncannot be reversed.

What Will Be Deleted?

Once your account is deleted, the following information will be permanently removed from FinanceHub.

Personal Profile

Name,email address,phone number,profile photo and account information.

Budget Records

All budgets,expenses,income records and spending history.

Financial Goals

Emergency fund tracker,savings goals and progress reports.

Investment Portfolio

Stocks,SIPs,mutual funds,gold,fixed deposits and investment history.

AI Advisor History

Personalized financial recommendations and AI chat history.

Reports

Downloaded reports,financial summaries and generated analytics.

Security Settings

Trusted devices,recovery options,login history and security preferences.

Notifications

Alerts,reminders,scheduled notifications and financial updates.

Before You Delete Your Account

We recommend completing these important steps before permanently deleting your FinanceHub account.

Download Your Reports

Keep a copy of your financial reports for future reference.

Export Financial Data

Save your budgeting and investment records before deletion.

Remove Trusted Devices

Sign out from devices you no longer use before deleting your account.

Cancel Active Reminders

Stop any recurring reminders and notifications associated with your account.
