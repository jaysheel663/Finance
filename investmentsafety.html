Investment Safety Guide

Learn safe investment practices,identify warning signs,and protect your money from fake investment schemes.

Safe Investment Principles

Follow these essential guidelines before investing your money.

Verify Registration

Always check whether the investment company is registered with SEBI,RBI,IRDAI or the appropriate government authority.

Research Before Investing

Read reviews,verify company details,and understand how the investment works.

Understand Risks

Every genuine investment carries some level of risk.There are no guaranteed high returns.

Read Documents

Never invest without reading official agreements,policies and investment terms.

Common Warning Signs

Avoid investments that show these red flags.

Guaranteed Returns

Promises like "Double your money in 30 days" are usually investment scams.

Urgent Pressure

Scammers force you to invest immediately without giving time to think.

Secret Investment

If someone tells you not to share the investment details with anyone,be cautious.

Unrealistic Profit

High profit with zero risk is never realistic.

Investment Safety Checklist

Complete these checks before investing.

Verified company registration.

Read investment documents.

Compared with other investment options.

Discussed with trusted family or financial advisor.

Understood risks and expected returns.

Never shared OTP or banking password.

Expert Investment Tips

Simple habits that help you stay financially safe.

Diversify Investments

Never invest all your savings in a single investment scheme.

Consult Experts

Seek advice from licensed financial professionals.

Protect Personal Data

Never share bank passwords,PINs or OTPs with investment agents.

Use Trusted Platforms

Invest only through trusted banks,government portals or registered brokers.
