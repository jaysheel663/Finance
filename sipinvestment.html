What is SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP)allows investors to invest a fixed amount regularly in Mutual Funds,helping create wealth through disciplined investing.

Small Investments,Big Future

SIP encourages financial discipline by investing a fixed amount every month,regardless of market conditions.

Over time,SIP benefits from rupee cost averaging and compounding,making it one of the most popular wealth creation methods.

Did You Know?

Investing ₹5,000 every month for many years can grow into a substantial corpus due to compounding.

Key Features of SIP

SIP offers flexibility,affordability and long-term financial growth.

Monthly Investment

Invest a fixed amount every month automatically.

Rupee Cost Averaging

Buy more units when prices are low and fewer when prices are high.

Power of Compounding

Returns generate additional returns over time.

Flexible Amount

Increase,decrease or stop SIP whenever needed.

Benefits of SIP Investment

SIP is one of the simplest and most effective ways to build wealth over the long term.

Affordable Investment

Start investing with as little as ₹500 every month.

Wealth Creation

Regular investing helps generate significant long-term wealth.

Rupee Cost Averaging

Risk Analysis

SIP itself is not risky.The investment risk depends on the Mutual Fund selected.

Market Risk

Fund values may increase or decrease with market conditions.

Short-Term Volatility

Temporary market declines are common during the investment journey.

Long-Term Stability

Staying invested longer usually reduces overall investment risk.

How to Start a SIP?

Starting a SIP is simple and requires only a few steps.Anyone can begin investing within minutes.

Complete your KYC using PAN,Aadhaar and bank account details.

Select a Mutual Fund that matches your financial goals and risk profile.

Decide your monthly SIP amount and investment date.

Enable Auto Debit from your bank account for uninterrupted investing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I stop my SIP anytime?

Yes.Most SIPs can be paused or stopped without any penalty.

Can I increase my SIP amount?

Yes.Many fund houses provide SIP Step-Up facilities.

Is SIP guaranteed?

No.SIP invests in Mutual Funds,so returns depend on market performance.
