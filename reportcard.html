Report Card Fraud

If you notice any suspicious debit or credit card activity,report it immediately to minimize financial loss and protect your account.

Common Card Frauds

Identify suspicious activities before reporting.

Unauthorized Transactions

Unknown purchases or withdrawals from your account.

Card Cloning

Your card information copied and used illegally.

Identity Theft

Someone using your personal information without permission.

Online Payment Fraud

Fraudulent online transactions or fake payment requests.

How to Report

1

Block Your Card

Immediately disable your card through your bank.

2

Collect Details

Keep transaction IDs, screenshots and SMS alerts ready.

3

Inform Your Bank

Contact customer care or visit your nearest branch.

4

File Complaint

Submit a fraud complaint with all available evidence.

Required Information

Transaction Details

Date,time,amount and merchant information.

Screenshots

Attach SMS alerts or banking app screenshots.

Identity Proof

Provide valid ID if requested by your bank.
