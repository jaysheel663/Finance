Common UPI Fraud Scenarios

These examples are fictional demonstrations based on common scam patterns.

Case 01

Fake QR Code Payment

A scammer tells the victim that scanning a QR code will deposit money into their account.The victim scans it and unknowingly authorizes a payment.

Safety Lesson Do not scan unknown QR codes to receive money.
Case 02

Fake Support Agent

A fraudster claims to be from a payment application's support team and asks the victim to follow instructions to resolve a supposed account problem.

Safety Lesson Use only official customer-support channels.
Case 03

Fake Cashback

The victim is promised cashback and is instructed to approve a payment request that actually sends money to the scammer.

Safety Lesson Check whether a UPI request is asking you to pay rather than receive money.
Case 04

Fake Buyer

A fake buyer claims to have paid or sends a misleading payment request and pressures the seller to authorize it.

Safety Lesson Confirm actual account credit instead of trusting screenshots.

Stop Before You Approve

Always check the recipient,amount,and transaction details before entering your UPI PIN.
