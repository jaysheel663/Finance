Complete Card Safety Guide

Protect your Debit and Credit Cards using industry best practices,learn secure payment habits and reduce fraud risks.

Card Safety Essentials

Follow these basic rules to protect your financial information.

Keep Your Card Safe

Never leave your card unattended or share it with anyone.

Protect Your PIN

Memorize your PIN and never write it on your card.

Cover the Keypad

Always cover the keypad while entering your PIN.

Enable Alerts

Receive instant SMS or app notifications for transactions.

ATM Safety

Use Trusted ATMs

Prefer ATMs located inside banks or secure locations.

Inspect ATM

Check for unusual card readers or hidden cameras.

Stay Alert

Be cautious if strangers offer help during transactions.

Online Payment Safety

HTTPS Websites

Shop only on trusted secure websites.

Official Apps Only

Download banking apps only from official app stores.

Never Share OTP

Your bank never asks for OTP or CVV.

Daily Safety Checklist

  • ✔Check transaction alerts regularly.
  • ✔Review bank statements every week.
  • ✔Lock your card if misplaced.
  • ✔Avoid public Wi-Fi for payments.
  • ✔Update banking passwords regularly.
  • ✔Report suspicious activity immediately.
