UPI Safety Checklist

UPI Fraud Safety Checklist

Use this checklist before approving a UPI payment or responding to an unexpected payment request.

Before Approving a UPI Payment

Quickly review these safety checks.

Verify the recipient

Confirm the recipient's name before authorizing payment.

Check the amount

Make sure the amount is exactly what you intended to pay.

Protect your UPI PIN

Never share your UPI PIN with another person.

Don't scan unknown QR codes

Never scan an unfamiliar QR code just to receive money.

Reject suspicious payment requests

Cancel requests that you did not initiate or expect.

Don't trust screenshots

Check your own account instead of relying on payment screenshots.

Use official support

Contact your bank or payment provider only through official channels.

Report suspicious activity

Report unauthorized or suspicious transactions promptly.

Keep This Checklist Handy

Print or save this checklist for quick reference whenever you use UPI.
